## Why one email for the whole group

All three websites belong to one company, [Xuzhou BOB-LIFT Construction Machinery Co., Ltd.](/about.html) If you buy more than one type of machine from us, for example a truck-mounted crane and a mini dumper for the same fleet, all your questions now go to the same inbox. You no longer need to remember which address belongs to which product line.

## How to send a crane enquiry that we can answer quickly

Whichever channel you use, these details help us reply with a usable answer first time:

- **The crane type and model**, or the capacity you need. Our [knuckle boom](/knuckle-boom-truck-mounted-cranes) and [telescopic boom truck-mounted cranes](/telescopic-boom-truck-mounted-cranes), [marine cranes](/knuckle-boom-marine-cranes) and [pickup cranes](/pickup-cranes) pages list current models.
- **Your truck or vessel**: chassis brand and model, or deck space and power for a marine crane.
- **The job**: typical load, outreach and working height.
- **Destination port or city** and the quantity, so we can include shipping in the quotation.
